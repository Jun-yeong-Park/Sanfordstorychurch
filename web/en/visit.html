I'm New

A few things
before you come.

You can come just as you are. Here are the things people usually wonder about.

What to expect

What is it like when I arrive?
Someone will meet you at the door. We are a small church, so a new face is noticed right away — but we will not put you on the spot. Say hello and take a seat, or stay and talk as long as you like.
How long is the service?
We start at 6 PM and finish within about an hour. Arriving ten minutes early gives you time to settle in. Afterwards we share a meal together — you are welcome to stay or head home.
What is the music like?
Led by Kanghyeon Heo. Not a large band — a simple set of songs chosen so everyone can sing along. That week's songs go up on the bulletin ahead of time if you would like to listen first.
What are the messages like?
Pastor Kyong Won Jung preaches straight through a passage, and takes it all the way to what it means for your week. There is a short time to share during the service so it does not end with listening.
What should I wear?
Whatever is comfortable. There is no dress code. Getting out the door on a Sunday evening is enough.
Can I bring my children?
Yes, please do. We do not have a separate children's program yet, so we would like to talk with you beforehand about how best to help during the service. Reach out before you come.
Do I have to give?
No. If you are visiting, please don't. When the basket comes around, just pass it along.
Will I have to introduce myself?
Only if you want to. You are welcome to sit, worship, and go.
