Denominational Affiliation

Sanford Story Church is part of the Christian Reformed Church in North America (CRCNA) — a denomination that traces its roots back to 1857 with Dutch immigrants who sought to faithfully live out the Reformed tradition. While honoring this rich heritage, the CRCNA has grown into a diverse, multi-ethnic family of churches across the United States and Canada. We are united by a deep commitment to biblical authority and a worldview that acknowledges God's sovereignty over every area of life.
