Worship

Sunday evening,
together.

We gather every Sunday at 6 PM. The service moves through four parts.

How the service flows

Our worship follows the same four pillars the church is built on. We listen, we share, we go deeper, and we go together.

  1. 01

    His StoryWelcome & Word

    Call to worship, singing, and the message. We begin by hearing the story of the One who saved us.

  2. 02

    Your StoryReflection & Sharing

    Write a note on what you heard, then share it with the person beside you — so it does not end with listening.

  3. 03

    Deep StoryPrayer

    We pray together, for the church and for one another.

  4. 04

    Our StoryResponse & Sending

    A closing song, the offering, and the benediction. Then we are sent back out.

The service runs about an hour. Afterwards we share a meal together.
