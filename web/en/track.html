Four Stories

Four steps into
God's story.

Know the church, share your story, go deeper, and walk together.

  1. 01

    His StoryKnow the church

    Hear what the gospel is, and get to know what kind of church this is.

  2. 02

    My StoryShare your story

    Share your own story, and become a member through baptism or profession of faith.

  3. 03

    Deep StoryGo deeper

    Learn Scripture and prayer until you can open the Bible on your own.

  4. 04

    Our StoryWalk together

    Join a small group and take one place on a serving team.

Once you have walked these four, you are the one welcoming the next person in. Tell us if you would like to join.

Four Things

And while we are together,
we do these four things.

What we have received does not stay with us. We serve, we give, we tell, and we learn so we can teach.

  1. Serving

    We serve

    Take one place — worship, media, welcome, hospitality. Not for those who are already good at it, but for those willing to learn while doing it.

    Serving teams →
  2. Giving

    We give

    So that worship and ministry can continue. Steadiness matters more than amount, and gladness most of all.

    Giving →
  3. Inviting

    We tell

    We hand the gospel we received to one person. You do not need the right words prepared — telling how your own life changed is the telling.

    For visitors →
  4. Learning

    We learn and teach

    Learning is not the end. We ask what this week's passage means in our own lives, and pass it on. The circle closes when the one who learned begins to teach.
